# A new game, from power-on to the player's room upstairs, then down the stairs and out into Pallet Town (Phase 3's
# step 1). Presses before the game has booted leave the screen white for good, so the route waits first. START at the
# name screens moves the cursor to OK, and A takes it
wait 900
repeat A/60 START/60 until field for 120 timeout 30000
check zone ZONE_ASPERTIA_CITY_4
# Beside the bed, the room's Fly position as the Kanto region sets it, not on the bed itself
check at 4 4
check player-visible
check var KANTO_VAR_STORY == KANTO_STORY_NEW_GAME
shot players-room
save players-room

# Around the room to the stairs, which the player steps onto from the right
walk DOWN 4, walk RIGHT 4, walk UP 4, walk RIGHT 2, walk UP 2, walk LEFT 1
until zone ZONE_ASPERTIA_CITY_3 timeout 600
until field for 60 timeout 600
check at 6 1
shot downstairs

# Out of the front door: onto the mat, then a step down off it
walk DOWN 10
check at 6 11
hold DOWN 30
until zone ZONE_ASPERTIA_CITY timeout 600
until field for 60 timeout 600
check player-visible
shot pallet-town
